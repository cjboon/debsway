Privacy is power. Keep yours.

For people who mean to live free. Wren Linux turns an ordinary computer into one that answers only to you: a disk sealed by a passphrase only you know, a browser walled off from your files, your keys, coins and conversations in your own hands, and every update refused unless it carries one signature. It is built to be hard to break and hard to get wrong, so that your freedom doesn't depend on being an expert.
